# Changelog

## Age-based government power penalties

- Ages II through VI now apply monthly penalties to Legitimacy, Republican Tradition, and Devotion.
- The penalty increases by 0.1 per age: -0.1 in Age II, -0.2 in Age III, -0.3 in Age IV, -0.4 in Age V, and -0.5 in Age VI.

## AI government power

- All AI difficulty settings now give AI countries +0.4 monthly Legitimacy, Republican Tradition, and Devotion.
